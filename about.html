Who We Are

Empowering Northwestern Texas residents to build communities that represent them through fact-based policies. We can shape our future together.

Our Mission in Hockley County

The Hockley County Democratic Party is dedicated to empowering the people of Northwestern Texas. We fight for economic opportunity, accessible healthcare, fully funded public schools, and a government that answers to working families, not special interests.

We organize year-round to elect leaders who share our values and to build a community-driven infrastructure that uplifts every neighborhood in Hockley County.

County Executive Committee

The County Executive Committee (CEC) conducts the official business of the party. It is composed of the County Chair and Precinct Chairs from across Hockley County.
